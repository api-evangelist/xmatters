# xMatters

> xMatters provides an incident response and workflow automation platform that helps DevOps, SRE, and IT operations teams detect issues, engage the right responders, and automate resolution workflows in real time. The platform connects monitoring, observability, collaboration, and ITSM systems to streamline operational response across complex technical environments.

## Key Differentiators
- Event-driven workflow automation that extends beyond basic alerting and paging to orchestrate operational response, remediation workflows, and cross-system coordination.
- Tight integration with monitoring, observability, and ITSM tools across the incident lifecycle.
- Designed for technical teams managing high-velocity systems and real-time operational events.
